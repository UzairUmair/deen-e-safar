# Manual image review
The final QA brief supersedes the earlier permission for blurred Prophet figures. No Prophet depiction, silhouette, back-facing figure or human stand-in is permitted. Prophetic stories use environments and objects only. The validator requires every approved Prophets asset to have a no-people review.

Inspect every generated image visually. Inspect human figures individually at original resolution, including corners, background faces and reflections. Reject recognizable facial features, authenticated-portrait claims, fabricated religious writing, violence and unsupported historical specificity. Sahaba and historical figures must remain anonymous with completely obscured faces and an illustration/unauthenticated-portrait disclosure.

Approval means thematic visual approval, never scholarly verification. Check scene relevance, cropping, alt text, decode, compression, dimensions and stable lesson mapping. A prompt or face detector is not visual approval. Record edits and rejected attempts; derivatives never count as new art. See final-image-quality-report.md for the completed review.
